Sidekick is an AI assistant that runs entirely on your phone. It sees what your camera sees, hears what you say, and remembers it for you — without sending any of it anywhere.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
• Everything is processed on your phone and stored there, encrypted.
• Works offline, even in airplane mode, once its AI models are downloaded.
• Online lookup is off until you turn it on. Even then, only the search words are sent.
• Export everything, or delete anything, any time. Uninstalling removes it all.

ASK ANYTHING
• Ask by voice, with a wake word, or by typing. Answers are spoken and shown in the chat.
• "What am I looking at?" — point the camera and ask.
• Hands-free when you're walking or driving: answers read aloud, and the headset button starts a question.
• Interpreter for travel, offline: "help me talk to this taxi driver in Spanish". You each speak your own language; Sidekick shows and says the translation, turn by turn.

REMEMBERS FOR YOU
• Remembers what you've seen and heard, so you can ask later: "What did that sign say this morning?"
• Search your memory by meaning, person, place or date.
• Keeps notes on the people you talk to, with facts you approve.
• Recaps of any time or topic: "recap what we worked on this week".
• Live notes of a conversation, updated as it goes.

GETS THINGS DONE — ALWAYS WITH YOUR OK
• Suggests reminders, to-dos and events from what you say; nothing is added until you approve it.
• Recurring reminders ("every Monday at 9") and an optional daily briefing.
• Drafts messages and emails in the chat; reads them back before sending, so you can change them.
• Schedule a message for later.
• Can read new messages aloud when you choose.
• Routines: "every day at 6 pm, recap my day", or when you arrive somewhere.
• "Navigate me to the station" opens directions straight away.
• Quiet at night: it won't speak out loud while you sleep or in Do Not Disturb.

CAMERA AND CONVERSATIONS
• Use the phone camera or a USB camera.
• Live transcript of conversations, with speakers told apart automatically.
• Consent mode, on by default where the law requires everyone's agreement: voices that haven't agreed are not kept.
• You always see when it's listening.

OPTIONAL EXTRAS (OFF UNTIL YOU TURN THEM ON)
• Online lookup through Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo, and news headlines.
• Google sign-in, used only for the feature you pick: send approved emails with Gmail, sync to-dos with Google Tasks, or back up to your Google Drive. Your phone talks directly to Google.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Sidekick uses a small AI model that fits on a phone. It is good at everyday help but is not an expert on deep specifics, and it can make mistakes.
• The first download is a few gigabytes; Wi-Fi is recommended. Download size depends on your phone.
• Needs enough free storage for the models; Sidekick checks before downloading.
