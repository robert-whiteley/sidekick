# Privacy

Sidekick is designed so that your data never leaves your phone. This is the technical detail; the user-facing policy (also shown in the app under Settings > About) is [docs/privacy-policy.md](docs/privacy-policy.md).

- **No network by default.** The only network use is downloading models (verified by SHA-256) from Hugging Face and GitHub. App updates come only through Google Play (the over-the-air update check is off: `updates.checkAutomatically: "NEVER"`). Your questions, photos, voice and memory are never sent anywhere unless you turn on Online lookup.
- **Online lookup is optional and off by default** (Settings > Online lookup). When on, and only for general or current-fact questions (not ones about you, your day or people you know, and never for photo questions), Sidekick sends a few search words for your question, with the names of people you know taken out, to Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation, `en.wikipedia.org`) and DuckDuckGo Instant Answers (`api.duckduckgo.com`), and to your own SearXNG server if you enter one. The search words are written on the phone by the on-device model, which sees only your question for this (not your memory or the conversation); if it is slow or fails, they are made from the question by fixed rules. For news questions ("latest", "today", "what happened"…) the search words also go to the GDELT Project (`api.gdeltproject.org`), and Sidekick downloads Wikipedia's "Current events" pages for today and yesterday and any news feeds you added in Settings (no question words are sent for these: the whole page or feed is downloaded and matched on the phone). Feeds must be https; none are added by default. Nothing else is sent: no memory, photos, voice, location, contacts or identifiers; no cookies; the User-Agent names only the app. These services see your IP address and the question words, as with any web request, under their own privacy policies. Results are clipped, used for that answer, and linked under it; the question and answer are kept in memory on the phone like any other. With no connection or a slow reply (over 4 s) the answer simply stays offline. The Ask screen shows "Offline" whenever lookup is off or there is no network. The "Search the web in your browser" link sends nothing until you tap it; then your browser opens DuckDuckGo with the search words (names taken out), under DuckDuckGo's policy.
- **Google features are optional and off by default** (ADR 0008). Each switch asks Google sign-in for one scope only when first turned on: `gmail.send` (send emails you approved; mail is never read), `tasks` (sync to-dos with Google Tasks), `drive.file` (upload the export file as a backup; Sidekick sees only files it created). Requests go straight from the phone to Google APIs; tokens stay on the phone.
- **No accounts, telemetry, analytics or ads.** No crash reporting in the MVP; any future crash reporting will be opt-in.
- **Encrypted storage.** Memory is stored in an SQLCipher-encrypted database; the key is kept in the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
- **Excluded from cloud backups**, so your memory is not copied to Google or Apple servers.
- **Recording is visible and controllable.** A permanent notification with a Stop button is shown while listening, and Android's mic indicator stays on. Conversation speech audio is kept on the phone for listening back (Storage sets how long), under the same consent rules as transcripts.
- **Location is optional and stays on the phone.** If you allow it, memories get coordinates and a town name looked up from a bundled offline list (GeoNames); Android's own location service is used, with no Google Play services and no online lookups. Deny it and everything else works.
- **Follow-ups need your approval.** To-dos, events and reminders Sidekick notices are only suggestions until you approve them. Reminders and the optional daily summary are local notifications scheduled on the phone (no push service, no server). An event goes into your calendar only through the phone's own "new event" screen, after you tap Calendar and then Save.
- **Data sources are off until you turn each one on.** Notes you type; text files (.txt, .md) in folders you pick with the system folder picker (Sidekick sees only those folders); contacts and calendar (read only, after the system permission prompt); and on Android the text of other apps' notifications (after you allow Notification access in system settings; one-time codes and media/progress/call notifications are skipped). Everything read is copied into the encrypted memory on the phone and nothing is sent anywhere. Delete everything one source added from Your data; that also turns it off.
- **Consent mode** (on by default everywhere) keeps other people's speech only after they agree out loud.
- **You own your data.** Export everything, or delete an item, a person, a time range, or all of it at any time.
