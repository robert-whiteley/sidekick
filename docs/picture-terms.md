## Google Play's rule

Google Play requires apps that make pictures with AI to prevent sexual content. Sidekick won't draw sexual or nude pictures.

## Reporting a picture

Every picture has a **Report** button. It opens your email app with a draft to the developer saying what was wrong and what was asked for. You read it and send it yourself; the picture isn't attached and nothing is sent without you.

## Privacy

Pictures are drawn on your phone. What you ask for and the pictures stay on your phone, in your chat history, unless you share or save them.
