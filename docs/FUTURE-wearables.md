# Future: Sidekick with a pendant or smart glasses

Notes for later (not this version). Researched 25 Nov 2026.

**Short answer:** nothing in Sidekick's design stops it. All these devices are "sensors + speaker"
that stream to a phone app over Bluetooth; Sidekick already does all thinking on the phone and already
accepts a USB camera and external mics. Each device needs its own connector (SDK or Bluetooth
protocol), and video over Bluetooth is low quality, so glasses would send photos or a few frames per
second, not full video.

## Blockers apart from SDKs

- **Bandwidth:** Bluetooth gives clear audio but only low-res or low-fps video. Use photos on demand
  or 1–2 fps frames; record full video only if the device stores it and syncs over Wi-Fi.
- **Battery:** streaming drains small devices fast (hours, not a day). Prefer "send on speech / on
  tap" over continuous streaming.
- **Vendor apps and rules:** Meta needs its app and its approval; Meta's policy leans against ambient
  recording. Open devices (Omi, Mentra, Brilliant, Even) have no such gate.
- **iOS:** background Bluetooth audio is possible, but Sidekick's iOS version is deferred.
- **Bystanders and law:** the same consent rules Sidekick uses for its own mic apply, plus a visible
  recording light on glasses.
- **Play policy:** no issue for Bluetooth devices (foreground service type `connectedDevice`, Nearby
  devices permission).

## What the app would need

1. An input "source" layer (exists: USB camera / mic choice) extended with Bluetooth sources.
2. Opus decoding to 16 kHz PCM into the existing speech pipeline (Silero VAD → Parakeet).
3. A frame source for glasses photos/frames into the existing camera memory.
4. Output to the device speaker (already routed via AudioRoutes) and, for display glasses, short text.
5. Per-device setup page in Settings → Camera & audio, and the Nearby devices permission on first use.

Start with **Omi** (open, MIT, audio only, cheapest) and **Mentra Live** or **Brilliant Halo** for
camera; Meta glasses last (closed SDK, telemetry, approval, policy fit).
