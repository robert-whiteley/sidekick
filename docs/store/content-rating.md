# Content rating (IARC questionnaire) — answers

Play Console → App content → Content rating. Category: **All other app types** (a utility or productivity app, not a game). Expected result: **PEGI 3 / Everyone / USK 0**, possibly with the "Users interact" or "Shares info" descriptors explained below.

| Question                                                                                  | Answer | Why                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Violence, blood, sexual content, nudity, crude humour, profanity in the app's own content | No     | The app has no built-in content. Answers come from an on-device model that is instructed to be helpful and safe; online lookup only quotes Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo and news headlines          |
| Controlled substances (drugs, alcohol, tobacco)                                           | No     | —                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Gambling, simulated gambling                                                              | No     | —                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content with other users?                | **No** | There's no chat between users and no social features. Messages and emails are handed to the user's own apps, or sent through their Gmail at their request, as with any keyboard or assistant |
| Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users?                 | No     | Location stays on the phone                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods?                                       | No     | Free; no in-app purchases                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Does the app contain ads?                                                                 | No     | —                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Is the app a web browser or search engine?                                                | No     | Online lookup is an optional answer source, not a general browser                                                                                                                            |
| Unrestricted internet access?                                                             | No     | The app only reaches the hosts listed in docs/store/data-safety.md; links open in the user's browser                                                                                         |

Target audience and content (App content → Target audience): **18+** only, or 16–17 and 18+. Do not include under-13s: it records audio and camera, and choosing children brings the Families policy. Answer "No" to "Could the app unintentionally appeal to children?".

Other App content items:

- Ads: No ads.
- News app: No (news headlines are an optional answer source).
- Health / financial features / government app: No.
- COVID-19 contact tracing: No.
- Data safety: docs/store/data-safety.md.
- Privacy policy URL: https://robert-whiteley.github.io/sidekick-site/privacy
- App access: "All functionality is available without an account. Some features (Gmail send, Google Tasks, Drive backup) need the reviewer's own Google account and are optional." No test login needed.
