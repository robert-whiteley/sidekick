# Feature audit: what other assistants do vs Sidekick

Research 25 Nov 2026. Compared: Gemini (Android), Siri / Apple Intelligence, Alexa+, ChatGPT app,
Perplexity Assistant (Android), Copilot, Meta AI, Rewind/Limitless, Omi, Bee, Plaud, Dicio, Google
AI Edge Gallery. "Have" was checked against `src/`, the native module, `docs/PLAN-v2.md` and ADR 0006
on this date. Policy notes refer to Google Play policy as read on this date.

Key: **Have** = built (phone-tested or not); **Part** = partly; **Gap** = missing.
Recommendation: **On** = on by default, **Off** = available but off by default, **Out** = leave out.
★ = top gap.

## 5. Proactive / background agents

What others do: **ChatGPT Tasks** (Jan 2025) runs a saved prompt at a time or repeatedly and notifies;
**Gemini Scheduled Actions** (Jun 2025, paid tiers, max 10 active) does the same; Alexa+ "routines"
and reminders; Bee and Limitless send daily summaries and suggested to-dos; Gemini/Pixel "Magic Cue"
surfaces info at the right moment.

## 7. Ideas from Dicio (GPL-3.0: ideas only, no code)

Dicio skills (README, Oct 2026): search (DuckDuckGo), weather (OpenWeatherMap), lyrics (Genius), open
app, calculator, telephone, timer, current time, navigation, jokes, media, translation (Lingva),
wake-word control ("stop listening"), read notifications, flashlight. It also does many languages
with hand-written sentence patterns and a settings page per skill.

Sidekick lacks: **weather**, **"stop listening" / "start listening" by voice** (check), **"what are my
notifications?" read all on demand** (it reads new ones only), **many languages**, and **per-skill
on/off switches**. Lyrics need a licensed source (Genius scraping is against its terms): leave out.

## Top gaps (in order)

1. Live conversation notes (update as it goes, no minimum length).
2. Recaps of any time range or topic, by request (replace the fixed "Your day").
3. User-made routines (scheduled and triggered) — replaces fixed briefing.
4. "Tell me when you see X" camera watch.
5. Weather (online lookup, with location).
6. Share into Sidekick.
7. Deep links for rides, shopping, video, music search (see app-actions.md).
8. Location timeline: places visited, "where did I park", place-based reminders.
9. QR / barcode scanning.
10. More languages (speech in/out).
11. Speech speed, "repeat that", "what can you do?".
12. Read all current notifications on demand; "stop listening" by voice.
13. Step count / activity (driving, walking) via Activity Recognition.
14. Note templates (meeting, lecture, doctor).
15. App shortcuts on the icon.
